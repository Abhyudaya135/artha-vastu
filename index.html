    Approvals roadmap

    The approvals a typical group housing or commercial project needs, arranged left to right in the order they are usually obtained. Lines show dependencies. Select any approval to see why it matters, how to get it, what it depends on, what it unlocks, and the documents to file.

    How to read confidence

    High means two or more independent sources agree. Medium means one primary source or a narrow set of deals. Indicative is a practitioner view where no clean public evidence existed; test it before relying on it. Statutory items should be checked on the state portal before closing.

    What updates on its own

    News is pulled live from Google News for whichever market you select, each time you open the page, change market, or every 30 minutes while it stays open. Assumptions, deals and trends come from the data edition shown at the top. If a file named artha-vastu-data.json sits next to this page on your web host, the page loads it automatically when it is newer, so refreshing the numbers is a single file swap. You can also load an updated file by hand below.

    Conventions and caveats

    Office rents are monthly on chargeable area, warm shell, excluding CAM, GST and parking. Residential prices are Anarock's basic selling price on super built-up area. Derived trend points are back-calculated from reported growth rates. Live headlines are third-party news, not verified by this book. Regulation summaries and approval checklists are a starting point for due diligence: always confirm against the current official text and the authority's own checklist. This is an analytical aid, not investment, legal or tax advice.
